Rane’s Sanskar Classes
No. 1 Coaching Classes In Santacruz

At Rane’s Sanskar Classes, we are committed to the future of your child. For over 29 years, we have endeavoured to achieve a perfect balance between knowledge and wisdom, individual attention and collective growth, and the present and future. Our aim is to shape your child into the leaders of tomorrow. Today, we are recognized as No. 1 coaching classes at Santacruz for SSC, Science, and Commerce. Our focus is on delivering results through a child-centric, service-oriented approach. The supportive environment we provide, our inherent culture, and our non-academic interventions all contribute to the overall academic improvement of our students. We firmly believe that Rane’s Sanskar Classes is fundamentally a family, not just an educational institution.

Our Mission

Education has been a fundamental aspect of our daily lives since ancient times. Our mission is to serve as a facilitator of the finest education at an affordable cost. We aim to extend our reach, providing quality coaching throughout the nation while establishing cutting-edge standards. Furthermore, we strive to ensure that every student achieves their professional and personal objectives through our platform.

Our Vision

Our vision is to establish ourselves as the foremost learning centre for students, preparing them comprehensively for the competitive world while instilling exemplary human values.
